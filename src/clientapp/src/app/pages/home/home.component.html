Welcome to your WireGuard management dashboard. Use the navigation drawer to monitor peers, manage your data, and configure the VPN server — all from one UI.

  • Monitor Peers — see live connection status and transfer statistics.
  • VPN Layout — visualise how targets, peer-groups and peers are linked.
  • Manage Data — add/edit/remove peers, peer-groups and targets.
  • Configuration — configure the VPN server and change your password.
